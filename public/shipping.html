Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated [date]

Where we deliver

We only deliver to addresses in India.

How we post

We post through India Post. Delivery times are estimates and depend on India Post. We are not responsible for delays caused by them.

  • Subscription letters go by standard India Post, without tracking. Letters start going out on 1 December 2026.
  • A letter to a friend goes by standard India Post, with a tracking number emailed once it’s posted.
  • A letter to your future self goes by standard India Post. It is posted to arrive around your chosen date, not on an exact date, and you’ll get a tracking number once it’s posted.
  • A Sign, by Post goes by Speed Post. It is dispatched within 3 working days, and you’ll get a tracking number once it’s posted.

Lost letters

If a letter doesn’t reach you, tell us within 30 days of the end of the month it was due. Once we’ve verified it, we’ll send a replacement in the following month. A replacement is the only remedy for a lost letter. We can’t replace letters lost because of a wrong or incomplete address you gave us.
