Privacy Policy

Last updated [date]

This policy explains what personal data Audacity collects through theaudacity.in, why we collect it, where it is kept and how you can ask us to delete it. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • When you book: your name, email, phone number and postal address, and the name and address of anyone you ask us to post a letter to.
  • What you give us for a letter: typed text, Word documents, voice notes, and your answers to our questions.
  • When you submit a confession: your audio or document, your email and, if you give it, a first name or nickname.
  • Payment details: Razorpay processes card, UPI, netbanking and wallet payments. We see the payment ID and amount, not your card or bank details. If you pay by UPI QR, we keep the UTR / transaction ID and any screenshot you upload.

Why we use it

To take your payment, write and post your letters, send you order and tracking emails, reply to you, and keep records the law requires. We use confessions only as described in our Terms and Conditions, without your name. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay, to process payments.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this site and stores orders and uploaded files in private storage.
  • Resend, which sends our emails.
  • Anthropic, only if you use the writing helper for a friend letter. Your three answers are sent to it to create a draft.
  • India Post, which sees the delivery name and address on the envelope.

We may also share data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order details for as long as we need them to deliver your letters and handle lost-letter claims, and after that only for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Letters to your future self are kept until they are posted.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email [email] and we’ll reply within 30 days. If we still need some of it to finish an order or meet a legal duty, we’ll tell you.

Grievances

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, write to our grievance contact at [email]. If we can’t resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
